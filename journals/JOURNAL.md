# 4/14/2026 2 PM - i FINALY finished the pcb design

_Time spent: 5.75h_

i finally finished designing the pcb and got the left side done properly, also i finished the edgecuts and added mouse bites. im going to order the pcbs soon. little suggestion for anyone else doing this project, dont even try to copy and flip one of the 2 sides on the pcb editor to make the other, its not possible, will take huge amounts of time and will turn out wrong. either make a oard that is flippable and is the same on both sides so that you dont have a left and right( this is great to save on shipping costs as well as production costs) or make one side and its edge cuts, then copy each row or collumn individual WITHOUT the traces, makeit look symetrical with the ruler tool and then route the traces and add the flipped edge cuts. dont make the same mistakes as me lol
